# Research Checklist

What to check before a sales meeting, organized by company type.

---

## Universal checklist (all company types)

### Website
- [ ] Homepage headline - clear or generic?
- [ ] CTA above fold - present? what does it say?
- [ ] Social proof - testimonials, logos, numbers?
- [ ] Case studies - specific results with client names?
- [ ] Contact options - phone, form, chat?
- [ ] Blog / resources - active? last post date?
- [ ] Pricing page - visible? hidden? contact-for-pricing?

### Tech signals
- [ ] CMS / platform (WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Shopify...)
- [ ] Analytics installed? (GA4, GTM visible)
- [ ] Meta Pixel installed?
- [ ] Chat tool present?
- [ ] Email capture / lead magnet?

### Marketing activity
- [ ] Search: is the company ranking for their own name + service terms?
- [ ] Ads: check Meta Ad Library for active ads
- [ ] Social: LinkedIn / Instagram / Facebook - last post date and engagement
- [ ] Blog: last post date, frequency

### Company context
- [ ] Years in business (About page, footer copyright)
- [ ] Team size signals (About page team section, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Recent news or announcements
- [ ] Job postings (reveal priorities and pain)
- [ ] Awards or certifications mentioned

## By company type

### Service business (agency, consultant, freelancer)

Extra checks:
- Number of case studies vs number of implied clients (ratio = credibility)
- Do they list industries served? (vertical vs generalist)
- Do they have a "process" or "how it works" page?
- Pricing: hourly / project / retainer model?
- Team: do they show photos? Names? Specializations?

Common pains to probe:
- "We're busy but not profitable" (pricing/positioning issue)
- "We get referrals but need more predictable lead flow" (marketing issue)
- "We pitch a lot but don't close enough" (social proof / positioning issue)
- "We have the clients but not the team to handle more" (scaling issue)

### eCommerce

Extra checks:
- Product photography quality (huge impact on conversion)
- Return/shipping policy visibility (above fold? footer?)
- Trust badges (secure checkout, certifications)
- Reviews visible on product pages?
- Upsell / cross-sell visible?
- Abandoned cart email? (check for Klaviyo, Mailchimp pixel)
- Loyalty or rewards program?

Common pains:
- "Our ad ROAS dropped and we don't know why" (attribution issue)
- "We have traffic but it's not converting" (CRO issue)
- "Our email list isn't converting" (sequence + segmentation)
- "We can't scale ads profitably" (margins + funnel issue)

### B2B company

Extra checks:
- Is the ICP (ideal customer profile) stated explicitly?
- Are there decision-maker-specific pages (e.g. "For CFOs", "For Operations")?
- Is there a demo / trial CTA?
- Do they have an ROI calculator?
- Integration page? (shows they're in a buyer's evaluation process)
- White papers, guides (indicates content-led sales motion)

Common pains:
- "Our sales cycle is too long" (trust + enablement issue)
- "Marketing generates leads but sales says they're not qualified" (funnel alignment)
- "We don't know where our best customers come from" (attribution / CRM)
- "We want to expand to a new market/vertical" (positioning + content)

### Local business (restaurant, clinic, gym, tradesperson)

Extra checks:
- Google Business Profile complete? (reviews, hours, photos)
- Address + phone above fold on website?
- Maps embed present?
- Online booking present?
- WhatsApp contact? (important in Spanish market)
- Schema markup for LocalBusiness?

Common pains:
- "We don't get found online for local searches" (Google Business + local SEO)
- "We have reviews but nobody's leaving new ones" (review generation)
- "We run Google Ads but it costs too much" (local campaign targeting)
- "We want to open a second location" (scaling digital presence)

## Hiring signal interpretation

Job postings reveal what the company is trying to fix or grow:

| Job posting | Interpretation |
|------------|---------------|
| Marketing Manager / CMO | Marketing currently underdeveloped or led by non-specialist |
| SEO Specialist | SEO is a pain point or high priority |
| Paid Ads Specialist | Scaling ads, or current ads not performing |
| Social Media Manager | Social not being done or underdelegated |
| Content Writer | Content strategy underway or needed |
| Web Developer | Website changes needed, likely rebuild or redesign |
| Sales Rep / BDR | Growing sales team, need lead support |
| CRM Admin | CRM implementation or cleanup needed |

## Quick background check sources

- Company website + /about
- LinkedIn company page (followers, employees, posts)
- Google: "[company name] review" or "[company name] news"
- Meta Ad Library: facebook.com/ads/library (no login needed)
- Google Business Profile: search company name
- Job postings: LinkedIn Jobs or their own website /careers

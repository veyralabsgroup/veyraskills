# Personalization Signals

What to look for when researching a prospect for cold outreach. Each signal maps to a personalization angle.

Personalization is real when the prospect reads it and thinks "they actually looked at my business." It is fake when it sounds like a mail merge.

---

## Website signals

| What you find | Personalization angle |
|--------------|----------------------|
| No Meta Pixel | "Your audience data is building in competitors' accounts" |
| No conversion tracking on GA4 | "You're running ads blind - no way to tie spend to leads" |
| No chat tool on high-traffic site | "Visitors who don't fill forms are leaving without a way to talk to you" |
| Wix / Squarespace on a B2B company | "Your platform may be limiting what you can track and optimize" |
| Outdated design (5+ years old signals) | "Your website may be sending the wrong signal to new visitors" |
| Generic H1 ("Welcome to Company") | "A visitor who doesn't already know you can't tell what you do in 3 seconds" |
| No case studies on a service business | "Prospects who visit can't see proof - that's a closing friction" |
| Feature-first copy (talks about "we") | "Your homepage talks about you, not what the client gets" |
| No blog or last post over 6 months old | "You're invisible in search to people who are actively looking" |
| Pricing hidden | "Forcing prospects to contact you for pricing increases drop-off" |

## Tech stack signals

| What you find | Personalization angle |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Running ads (pixel found) but no retargeting audiences setup | "You're paying for clicks without capturing the ones who didn't convert" |
| GA4 installed but no goals configured | "You have analytics but no conversion data - you can't optimize what you can't measure" |
| WordPress with outdated theme / many plugins | "Your site is likely slowing down every time someone loads it" |
| No email capture or newsletter | "You're losing every visitor who's interested but not ready to buy yet" |
| HubSpot installed (free) | They're thinking about CRM / marketing automation |

## Activity signals

| What you find | Personalization angle |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Inactive social (3+ months no posts) | "Your social profiles are the first thing a prospect checks after visiting your site" |
| Running ads with thin or stock creative | "Ads that look like ads get scrolled past - creative is the main lever now" |
| Blog with thin content (under 300 words per post) | "Google's ranking algorithm now rewards depth - short posts rarely rank" |
| High Google Ads spend visible (many ads in library) | They're investing in paid - focus on efficiency, not whether to do it |
| No Google Ads but competitors are running them | "Competitors are capturing searches for your services while you're not present" |

## Company context signals

| What you find | Personalization angle |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Recently hired a marketing role | They're scaling marketing - moment to offer supporting infrastructure |
| New product or service launched (from blog/news) | "Launching something new is the moment when the right acquisition setup makes the difference" |
| Recent rebrand or website update | "After a rebrand is when you want to make sure the tracking is set up correctly" |
| Expanding to new market or location | "New market means new audience - what's the plan for reaching them?" |
| Awards or certifications mentioned | Use as positive opener before pivoting to the gap |
| Many positive reviews but not visible on site | "You have the proof - the opportunity is making it visible to people who haven't heard of you yet" |

## LinkedIn signals (if contact profile available)

| What you find | Personalization angle |
|--------------|----------------------|
| New to role (under 6 months) | New leaders often want early wins to establish credibility |
| Posted about a specific challenge | Use their own words as the hook |
| Promoted recently | Expanded responsibility = expanded budget authority often |
| Worked at a well-known brand before | "Having seen how [big brand] does it, you know what good looks like" |
| Shared content about AI tools | They're interested in efficiency and new tools |

## What real personalization looks like

**Fake (template):**
> "I was impressed by your company's work in the [industry] space."

**Real:**
> "I noticed you're running Meta Ads but your Pixel isn't tracking any conversion events - which means Meta is optimizing for clicks, not for the people who actually contact you."

The difference: the real version requires actually looking at the site. The fake version could apply to anyone.

---

## Personalization depth tiers

**Tier 1 (easy, takes 2 minutes):**
- Company name in subject line
- Mention their platform or tool
- Reference a specific page on their site

**Tier 2 (medium, takes 5 minutes):**
- Specific technical observation (no pixel, no conversion tracking)
- Reference a specific competitor they should care about
- Comment on their social activity or blog

**Tier 3 (deep, takes 10-15 minutes):**
- Quote their own copy back at them ("Your site says X, but prospects want to hear Y")
- Reference a recent blog post or announcement
- Connect their LinkedIn activity to the outreach angle
- Show them a gap vs a direct competitor with evidence

For cold outreach at scale, Tier 2 is the sweet spot. Tier 3 is for high-value targets or inbound follow-up.

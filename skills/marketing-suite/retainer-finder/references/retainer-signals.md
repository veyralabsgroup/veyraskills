# Retainer Signals

Signal library for retainer-finder. Maps observable company signals to recurring service opportunities.

---

## Signal categories

### Signals that indicate active depreciation (highest priority)

These mean money is being lost right now without ongoing management:

| Signal | What is depreciating | Retainer opportunity |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Ads running (pixel detected) | ROAS decaying without optimization | Ads management retainer |
| Blog active but old posts (3+ months) | Rankings dropping as competitors publish | SEO content retainer |
| Email tool detected but no send history | List decaying, deliverability at risk | Email marketing management |
| Social profiles with 1000+ followers, inactive | Audience connection fading | Social media management |
| WordPress site (especially with many plugins) | Security vulnerabilities accumulating | WordPress maintenance |
| GA4 installed but no goals | Attribution breaking with every platform update | Analytics management |
| Google Business Profile with reviews unanswered | Trust signal declining | Local SEO management |

### Signals that indicate latent retainer need (medium priority)

These do not have active depreciation yet, but create it once implemented:

| Signal | What to implement | Why it becomes a retainer |
|--------|------------------|--------------------------|
| No email list | Email capture + welcome sequence | Once built, needs ongoing sends and list management |
| No paid ads but budget signals exist | Campaign setup | Once running, needs ongoing optimization |
| No Google Business Profile | GBP setup | Once created, needs monthly post + review management |
| Thin blog (under 10 posts) | Content strategy | SEO takes 3-6 months minimum to show results |
| No social presence | Account setup + initial content | Social algorithms punish inactivity |

### Signals that reduce retainer likelihood

| Signal | Why it reduces probability |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Already has an in-house marketing team | Internal capacity reduces agency dependency |
| Freelancer-run business (solo operator) | Budget constraints, less likely to commit to monthly |
| Recently signed with another agency | Switching cost, loyalty |
| Static website with no marketing activity | May not value ongoing marketing |
| Industry with very long sales cycles (e.g. industrial B2B) | Harder to show monthly ROI |

## Retainer packages by company profile

### SMB service business (agency, consultant, clinic, law firm)

Typical pain: inconsistent leads, no online presence management
Best retainer stack:
- SEO content (2-4 articles/month) - 500-1200€/month
- Google Business management - 200-400€/month
- Monthly reporting - included or 150€/month
Total MRR: 700-1600€

Entry project: Analytics setup + quick wins audit (300-500€)
Results moment: "We can now see where your leads come from - want us to act on it?"

### eCommerce (Shopify, WooCommerce)

Typical pain: ROAS declining, email underused, cart abandonment
Best retainer stack:
- Meta/Google Ads management - 400-800€/month + % of spend
- Email flows management (Klaviyo/Mailchimp) - 400-600€/month
- Monthly analytics report - 150-250€/month
Total MRR: 950-1650€ + % of ad spend

Entry project: Pixel setup + conversion tracking audit (300-500€)
Results moment: "Your pixel is now tracking add-to-carts and purchases - we can see which campaigns are actually profitable."

### B2B company (software, professional services)

Typical pain: long sales cycle, content thin, no lead nurturing
Best retainer stack:
- SEO content strategy + writing - 800-1500€/month
- LinkedIn content management - 400-700€/month
- Email nurture sequence management - 300-500€/month
Total MRR: 1500-2700€

Entry project: Content audit + keyword research (500-800€)
Results moment: "Your top 3 service pages now rank on page 1 - want us to build on that?"

### Local business (restaurant, gym, clinic, tradesman)

Typical pain: not found locally, reviews unmanaged, social inactive
Best retainer stack:
- Google Business management + local SEO - 300-500€/month
- Social media management (2-3 posts/week) - 400-700€/month
- Monthly local ads (if budget exists) - 200-400€/month management
Total MRR: 700-1600€

Entry project: Google Business setup + audit (200-300€)
Results moment: "You're now appearing in the map pack for [search term] - want us to keep that position?"

## Dependency scoring (1-10)

Score each service by how hard it is for the client to remove the agency:

| Service | Dependency score | Reason |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| Paid ads management | 9 | Campaign history, audience data, optimization knowledge |
| SEO content (ongoing) | 8 | Stopping kills rankings built over months |
| Email automation | 8 | Flows, segments, suppressions - complex to hand off |
| Analytics management | 7 | Interpretation layer, custom dashboards |
| Social media management | 6 | Brand voice, content calendar - can be internalized but painful |
| WordPress maintenance | 6 | Fear of breaking things, security knowledge |
| Google Business management | 5 | Can be done in-house but often isn't |
| Reporting only | 4 | Easy to replicate with templates |
| One-time SEO audit | 1 | Delivered, done, no ongoing hook |

Target retainer stacks should have at least one service scoring 7+ to ensure renewal.

## Renewal risk patterns

| Risk | Early signal | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Client hires in-house marketer | Job posting for marketing role | Position agency as specialist layer above generalist hire |
| Budget cuts | Company slowing hiring, news of downturn | Document ROI clearly every month, tie spend to revenue |
| Expectations mismatch | Asks for results before timeline | Set realistic milestones in month 1, show leading indicators |
| Key contact leaves | Their champion changes role or company | Build relationship with 2+ stakeholders |
| Results plateau | Agency shows same metrics month after month | Regularly introduce new opportunities to keep value fresh |

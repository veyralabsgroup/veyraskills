# Quick Wins

20 common quick wins for agency audits. Each takes under 1 working day and produces a visible, measurable result.

Used in Phase 3 to populate the Quick Win section of the Problem Map.

---

## SEO Quick Wins

**QW-S1: Write meta descriptions for top pages**
- Time: 2-3 hours for 10 pages
- What: Add unique 120-160 char meta descriptions with a benefit and action
- Result: Improved click-through rate in search results
- Applies when: Meta descriptions missing or auto-generated

**QW-S2: Fix the title tag on the homepage**
- Time: 30 minutes
- What: Rewrite from "Home | Company" to "[Service] in [City/Market] | [Brand]"
- Result: Keyword signal improves, search snippet more relevant
- Applies when: Title tag is generic or missing primary keyword

**QW-S3: Add H1 to pages missing it**
- Time: 30 minutes per page
- What: Add a single H1 that matches the page intent and primary keyword
- Result: Search engine understands page topic correctly
- Applies when: H1 missing or duplicate H1s on same page

**QW-S4: Add alt text to key images**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Write descriptive alt text for hero images, product images, team photos
- Result: Accessibility compliance + image search indexing
- Applies when: Alt text missing or empty on main images

**QW-S5: Submit sitemap to Google Search Console**
- Time: 30 minutes (if GSC already set up)
- What: Create XML sitemap if missing, submit via GSC
- Result: Faster indexing of new/updated pages
- Applies when: No sitemap found or not submitted

## Analytics Quick Wins

**QW-A1: Install Google Tag Manager**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Add GTM container to all pages, migrate GA4 tag inside
- Result: Future tags deployed without developer access
- Applies when: Tags implemented directly in HTML, no GTM present

**QW-A2: Set up GA4 goal tracking**
- Time: 2-3 hours
- What: Create conversions for form submissions, calls, purchases
- Result: Traffic sources can be tied to actual leads
- Applies when: GA4 installed but no conversions configured

**QW-A3: Install Meta Pixel with PageView event**
- Time: 1 hour
- What: Add Meta Pixel ID via GTM or direct code
- Result: Enables retargeting audiences immediately (audience builds from day 1)
- Applies when: Meta Pixel not detected, client wants to run Meta Ads

**QW-A4: Install Google Ads conversion tag**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Add Google Ads conversion action for form or call
- Result: Campaign optimization data, smart bidding enabled
- Applies when: Running or planning Google Ads with no conversion tracking

## Conversion Quick Wins

**QW-C1: Rewrite homepage CTA**
- Time: 30 minutes
- What: Change "Submit" / "Send" / "Contact" to action + benefit ("Get a free audit", "Request a quote", "Book a 20-min call")
- Result: Higher form submission rate
- Applies when: CTA is generic, passive, or unclear

**QW-C2: Add phone number to header**
- Time: 30 minutes
- What: Place phone number visibly in site header, clickable on mobile
- Result: Lowers barrier for inbound calls, trust signal
- Applies when: Phone number buried in footer or contact page only

**QW-C3: Add social proof section to homepage**
- Time: 2-4 hours
- What: Add 2-3 testimonials with name, company, and result; or client logo strip
- Result: Trust signal increases conversion rate
- Applies when: No testimonials or social proof visible on homepage

**QW-C4: Make contact form visible without scrolling**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Move or duplicate CTA/form above the fold
- Result: Reduces drop-off from visitors who don't scroll
- Applies when: First CTA is below fold on desktop and mobile

**QW-C5: Add a clear value proposition to the homepage**
- Time: 1-2 hours (copy only, no redesign)
- What: Rewrite H1 and subheadline to answer: who is this for, what do they get, why here
- Result: Bounce rate reduction, higher engagement
- Applies when: Homepage headline is vague or company-name-only

## Performance Quick Wins

**QW-P1: Add loading="lazy" to images**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Add lazy loading attribute to below-fold images in HTML/CMS
- Result: Faster initial page load, better LCP score
- Applies when: Images not using lazy loading

**QW-P2: Add defer/async to non-critical scripts**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Add defer attribute to analytics and chat scripts in <head>
- Result: Reduces render-blocking, faster Time to Interactive
- Applies when: Multiple scripts in <head> without defer/async

**QW-P3: Compress and convert hero image to WebP**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Re-export hero/banner images as WebP at appropriate dimensions
- Result: 30-70% smaller image files, faster LCP
- Applies when: Hero images are JPG/PNG and over 200KB

## Content Quick Wins

**QW-T1: Add a case study or result stat to homepage**
- Time: 2-4 hours
- What: Add one specific result ("Helped X achieve Y in Z months") with client name if possible
- Result: Credibility + specificity that generic copy cannot match
- Applies when: Homepage has no specific results or social proof

**QW-T2: Add FAQ section to key service pages**
- Time: 2-4 hours per page
- What: Write 5-8 common questions + answers for the main service
- Result: Long-tail keyword coverage + reduces pre-sales objections
- Applies when: Service pages have thin content and no FAQ

**QW-T3: Fix broken links**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- What: Crawl site for 404s (Screaming Frog free tier or manual), fix or redirect
- Result: User experience + crawl efficiency
- Applies when: Pages have been deleted/moved without redirects

**QW-T4: Add opening hours and address to footer**
- Time: 30 minutes
- What: Add structured address + hours to footer (and schema markup if time allows)
- Result: Local SEO signal + basic trust for local business
- Applies when: Local business with no visible address or hours on site

---

## Quick Win Selection Rules

When choosing which quick wins to include in a proposal:
1. Pick 3-4 maximum (more dilutes impact)
2. Prioritize those with measurable results (CTR, form rate, load time)
3. Include at least one that is visible to the client (design/copy) and one that is invisible but important (tracking/SEO)
4. Match to the problems already flagged - quick wins should feel like "we start here while the bigger work gets set up"

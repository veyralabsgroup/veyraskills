## Service Priority Rules

1. **Entry-door services first** - analytics, pixel, GTM. Cheap, fast, creates dependency. Client cannot evaluate campaign results without them.

2. **Prerequisites matter** - never pitch Ads to a client with no conversion tracking. Flag the dependency explicitly.

3. **Quick wins as proof of concept** - when pitching a retainer, bundle 2-3 quick wins in week 1. Client sees results before full engagement starts.

4. **Recurring vs project** - prioritize services that convert to monthly retainers. Projects are cash, retainers are business.

5. **Match to what the agency delivers** - if the agency does not do email marketing, do not include it in the proposal. Flag it as "outside our scope, recommend [type of partner]" instead.
